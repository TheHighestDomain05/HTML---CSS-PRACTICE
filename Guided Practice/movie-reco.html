Movie Recommendations by Robert Arboleda

My top 3 movies of all-time

Interstellar

by Christopher Nolan

Genre: Sci-fi, Drama, Epic, Dystopian

Joseph Cooper (Matthew McConaughey) a widowed former NASA pilot turned farmer
Observed a mysterious gravitational anomalies in Murph's bedroom (daughter)
lead Cooper to a secret remnant of NASA, led by Professor Brand (Michael Caine)

The goal of the story is to solve the theory of quantum gravity to harness
gravitational propulsion. This would allow humanity to launch massive space
stations carrying the remaining population off Earth and relocate them to a new,
habitable planet.

Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle Part 1

by Koyoharu Gotouge

Genre: Action, Dark Fantasy, Supernatural, Martial Arts

Following the explosive climax of the Hashira Training Arc, the Demon King Muzan Kibutsuji
ambushes the Demon Slayer Corps, seeking to devour Nezuko and gain immunity to sunlight.
After Corps leader Kagaya Ubuyashiki sacrifices himself to trap Muzan, the remaining Hashira
and Demon Slayers plunge into a desperate counterattack. However, Muzan’s subordinate
Nakime shifts reality around them, pulling the entire Corps into the Infinity Castle—an endlessly
shifting, gravity-defying dimensional fortress.

With time running out and the survival of humanity on the line, the Demon Slayers must unlock
their inner limits, uncover ancient breathing techniques, and make the ultimate sacrifices to
eliminate Muzan’s elite guard, destroy the Demon King himself, and survive until sunrise.

Rush Hour: Trilogy

by Ross LaManna

Genre: Buddy cop, Action, Martial arts comedy

The Rush Hour trilogy follows the explosive partnership of Inspector Lee (Jackie Chan),
a disciplined Hong Kong martial artist, and Detective James Carter (Chris Tucker), a
fast-talking LAPD officer. Brought together in LA to rescue a diplomat's kidnapped
daughter, the mismatched duo overcomes cultural clashes and language barriers using
Lee’s acrobatic fight skills and Carter’s street smarts to dismantle the crime ring.

Their adventures expand globally in the sequels, taking them from Hong Kong and Las Vegas
to Paris as they battle counterfeit money rings and top Triad crime lords. Through
high-octane martial arts, high-stakes chases, and non-stop comedic banter, Lee and
Carter evolve from reluctant partners into an inseparable, fiercely loyal brotherhood.
